# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — first public beta
- First public beta for WoW Forever (Interface 16001).
- Time per level and zone, split by activity: on foot, mounted, flight path, swimming, standing, combat, AFK, dead/ghost, town (vendor, mailbox, auction house, bank, trainer, flight master), crafting, gathering, eating and drinking.
- XP per level and zone from quests, mob kills (with rested and group bonus shown apart) and exploration, plus XP per hour.
- Dashboard with tiles (time, XP, XP/hour, kills, quests, deaths), bar and line charts, filters, click-to-drill and a one-click character overview.
- Death log with a 15-second health recap, your target, and the cooldowns and healing potions you still had ready.
- All characters on your account in one dropdown. Minimap button. `/ledger`, `/levelledger`, `/ll`.
- All data stays local; nothing is sent anywhere.
